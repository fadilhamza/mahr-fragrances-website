Shipping & Returns

Shipping

We offer complimentary shipping across India on every order via our logistics partner. Orders are processed within 24–48 hours and typically arrive within 4–7 business days. You'll receive tracking details by email and WhatsApp as soon as your order ships.

Returns & Exchanges

Unopened, unused products can be returned within 7 days of delivery for a full refund. Due to the nature of fragrance products, opened bottles cannot be returned unless received damaged or incorrect. To start a return, contact our support team with your order number.

Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If your order arrives damaged or you receive the wrong fragrance, reach out within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the product and packaging, and we'll arrange a free replacement or refund.
